## Profile

Masaya Ochiai is a system trader by background and an independent researcher based in Japan.

His research examines knowledge-production systems, including scholarly publishing, artificial intelligence, and financial markets, through systems thinking and quantitative perspectives developed through professional practice in algorithmic trading. His work connects philosophy, ethics, social epistemology, and the governance of institutions that produce, filter, and circulate knowledge.

His current research focuses on philosophy of finance, social epistemology, the epistemology of financial markets, and the conditions under which publication changes what can be known.
